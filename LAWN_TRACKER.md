## 📖 Detailed Timeline (March → November)

<a id="step-prep"></a>
### 🧹 Prep Tasks
**Timing:** Early → Mid March  **Programs:** Both
**Status:** ❌ Skipped

**What to do:**
- **Pick your spring path:**
  - **Seed Safe path** if you plan to seed thin or bare areas this spring. It runs Renovator (1A), then Seed Safe (2A).
  - **Idiot-Proof path** if you're not seeding. It runs PREVENT! (1B), then Weed & Feed (2B).
  - You can't do both: PREVENT! stops grass seed from sprouting too.
- **Equipment:** sharpen mower blades, change the oil and plug, and calibrate the spreader (check the setting chart on each bag).
- **Cleanup:** rake out leaves, sticks and matted snow-mold patches.
- **Optional:** run a soil test through K-State or UNL Extension. It tells you pH and whether you need lime.
- **Don't** aerate or power-rake now if you're on the Idiot-Proof path. It disturbs the pre-emergent barrier.

---

<a id="step-1a"></a>
### 🌾 Step 1: Renovator®
**Timing:** Early → Mid March  **Programs:** Seed Safe only
**Status:** ➖ N/A (Idiot-Proof path chosen)

**What to do:**
- Apply Renovator® at the bag rate. It feeds the existing turf and gives food to the seed you'll put down.
- Water it in (about ¼") or apply before rain.
- Set a reminder **~3 weeks out** for Seed Safe (Step 2A). GrassPad specifies that gap.
- Prep the spots you'll seed: rake bare areas down to loose soil.

---

<a id="step-1b"></a>
### 🛡️ Step 1: PREVENT!®
**Timing:** Mid-March → Mid-April  **Programs:** Idiot-Proof only
**Status:** ❌ Skipped

**What to do:**
- Apply PREVENT!® (crabgrass pre-emergent + fertilizer) at the bag rate.
- **Timing cue:** apply before soil holds at 55°F (the *Lawn* tab in this app shows soil temperature), or when forsythia bushes are in bloom. Earlier in the window is safer than later.
- Water in with ~½" within a few days. That activates the barrier.
- **No seeding, aerating or dethatching** after this goes down. It breaks the barrier and blocks seed.
- It's safe for kids and pets once watered in and dry.

---

<a id="step-w1"></a>
### 💧 Spring Watering & Mowing Setup
**Timing:** Early → Mid April  **Programs:** Supplemental
**Status:** ❌ Skipped

**What to do:**
- **Mowing:** first cuts at **3"**, removing no more than ⅓ of the blade per cut. Mulch the clippings.
- **Watering:** usually none needed. Spring rain covers it. Only water if you go 10+ days dry: ~1"/week total, in 1–2 deep sessions, early morning.
- **Irrigation:** turn the system on, check for broken heads, and set it to **manual / off** for now so it doesn't overwater in spring.
- **If you're on the Seed Safe path and seeded:** keep seed moist with light watering 2–3× daily until it germinates.

---

<a id="step-2a"></a>
### 🌱 Step 2: Seed Safe®
**Timing:** Mid-April (~3 weeks after Renovator)  **Programs:** Seed Safe only
**Status:** ➖ N/A (Idiot-Proof path chosen)

**What to do:**
- Apply Seed Safe® (seed-safe crabgrass pre-emergent + fertilizer, mesotrione-based) at the bag rate.
- Seed the same day or around it. Seed Safe stops crabgrass but lets your new grass come up.
- Water lightly **2–3× daily** until germination (7–14 days for fescue), then taper to deeper, less frequent watering.
- Keep foot traffic off the seeded areas.
- **Don't** use Weed & Feed or other broadleaf killers on new grass until it's been mowed 3–4 times.

---

<a id="step-2b"></a>
### 🌼 Step 2: Weed & Feed
**Timing:** April → Early June  **Programs:** Idiot-Proof only
**Status:** ❌ Skipped

**What to do:**
- Apply when dandelions and other broadleaf weeds are **actively growing and flowering**.
- **Apply to wet grass** (morning dew, or after a light sprinkle) so the granules stick to the weed leaves.
- **Don't water or mow for 24–48 hours** after. Check the forecast for a dry 24 hours first.
- Best results: 60–85°F days, light wind.

---

<a id="step-s1"></a>
### 🐛 Grub Preventer
**Timing:** Mid-May → Late June  **Programs:** Supplemental (recommended)
**Status:** ❌ Skipped

**What to do:**
- Apply a preventive grub control. **Chlorantraniliprole** (e.g. GrubEx) is the lowest-toxicity option and lasts the whole season.
- Water in with ~½" within 24 hours.
- **Why:** Japanese beetle and masked chafer grubs hatch in July and eat roots in August–September. Preventing them is far cheaper than repairing dead patches. It also protects any fall seeding.
- **Skip** if you've never had grub damage and don't see Japanese beetles. Instead, just do the August check (S4).

---

<a id="step-s2"></a>
### 🦟 Mosquito Season Start
**Timing:** Mid-May, then repeat every 3–4 weeks through September  **Programs:** Supplemental (optional)
**Status:** ❌ Skipped

**What to do:**
- **Free first step:** dump standing water weekly (buckets, saucers, gutters, toys, tarps). This does more than spraying.
- Put **Bti dunks** in water you can't dump (bird baths, rain barrels, ponds). They're safe for pets and wildlife. Replace them monthly.
- **Optional barrier spray** (bifenthrin, or a botanical such as cedar oil) on shrubs, under decks and in shady foliage. Don't spray the open lawn or flowering plants, which protects bees.
- **Re-spray every 3–4 weeks.** I'll remind you: June, July, August and early September rounds.

---

<a id="step-3"></a>
### 🛡️ Step 3: PREVENT!® (2nd Application)
**Timing:** Late May → Early July (GrassPad's sweet spot is **late May – early June**)  **Programs:** Both
**Status:** ❌ Skipped

**What to do:**
- Apply PREVENT!® at the bag rate. This is round two of crabgrass prevention, carrying the barrier through summer.
- Water in with ~½".
- **If you seeded this spring (Seed Safe path):** only apply once the new grass has been mowed **3+ times**.
- **If you plan to overseed in fall:** apply by **mid-June** at the latest. A later application can still be active in September and stop your seed.

---

<a id="step-w2"></a>
### ☀️ Summer Mode: Mowing & Watering
**Timing:** Late May → August  **Programs:** Supplemental
**Status:** ❌ Skipped

**What to do:**
- **Raise the mower to 3.5–4"** by Memorial Day. Tall grass shades out weeds and holds moisture.
- **Watering:** **1–1.5"/week** total (rain + irrigation), in **2–3 deep sessions**, **4–9 AM** only. Evening watering invites brown patch.
  - Tuna-can test: set a can out, and run the sprinkler until it holds 1". That's your run time.
- **No fertilizer June–August.** Feeding cool-season grass in heat feeds disease. Steps 3 and 4 have it covered.
- **Heat waves (95°F+):** keep off the lawn when it's stressed. It's OK to let tall fescue go dormant, but give ½" every 2 weeks so the crowns survive.
- **Mower blade:** resharpen around July 4. Dull blades shred the tips and invite disease.

---

<a id="step-s3"></a>
### 🍄 Brown Patch Watch / Fungicide
**Timing:** June → August  **Programs:** Supplemental (optional)
**Status:** ❌ Skipped

**What to do:**
- **Watch for:** tan, roughly circular patches 6"–3' across, often with a dark "smoke ring" at the edge on humid mornings. This shows up mostly on tall fescue.
- **Trigger:** nights above 68–70°F combined with humidity or evening watering.
- **Prevention:** water only in the early morning, don't fertilize in summer, and mow with a sharp blade.
- **Fungicide (optional):** if you've had brown patch before, apply azoxystrobin or propiconazole preventively in mid-June and repeat at label intervals (14–28 days) through August.

---

<a id="step-s4"></a>
### 🔍 Grub Check & Rescue Treatment
**Timing:** Mid-August → Mid-September  **Programs:** Supplemental (only if needed)
**Status:** ❌ Skipped

**What to do:**
- **Check:** in any brown patch, see whether the turf peels back like carpet. If it does, cut and lift a 1 sq ft piece of sod.
- **10+ grubs per sq ft** → apply a curative (**trichlorfon**, e.g. Dylox) right away and water it in. It works within days.
- **Fewer than 10** → no treatment. Healthy grass outgrows them.
- If you applied S1, this is mostly a quick confirmation check.
- Skunks, raccoons or birds digging up the turf is a strong grub sign.

---

<a id="step-4"></a>
### 🍂 Step 4: Renovator®
**Timing:** September → October  **Programs:** Both
**Status:** ⬜ Not started

**What to do:**
- Apply Renovator® at the bag rate. It's a strong nitrogen dose that helps the lawn recover from summer stress.
- **If you're doing Fall Renovation:** apply on seeding day (3–5 lb/1,000 sq ft) as the starter fertilizer. One application covers both.
- Water in with ~¼–½".
- Early September is ideal if you're not seeding. It gives the longest recovery window.

<a id="step-fr"></a>
### 🌾 Fall Renovation / Overseeding
**Timing:** Prep late August → seed **Sep 1 – Oct 10** (best by **Sep 30**; the earlier, the better root depth before winter)  **Programs:** Optional
**Status:** ⬜ Not started

**What to do (in order):**
1. **Late August:** kill weeds and crabgrass in the areas to renovate. Wait the herbicide label's interval before seeding.
2. **Mow 1–2 notches lower** (~2.5–3" for fescue or bluegrass). Don't scalp.
3. **Remove debris and clippings.** Seed needs bare soil contact.
4. **Rough up the soil:** rake bare spots hard. For large areas, **core aerate or verticut/slit-seed** (rent the machine).
5. **Spread seed** over the whole area with a rotary or hand-crank spreader. Overseed rate is on the bag (typically 4–6 lb/1,000 sq ft for tall fescue).
6. **Apply Renovator®** at the fall rate, **3–5 lb/1,000 sq ft**, as your starter fertilizer. This **is** Step 4, so mark both done.
7. **Water lightly 3× a day for 3 weeks** (see W3). **Don't stop once you've started.** Seed that dries out after sprouting dies.
8. First mow when the new grass reaches ~4", cutting at 3".
9. **No weed killer** on the new grass until it's been mowed 3–4 times (so skip S5 this year).
10. Rake leaves off the new grass promptly (S6). Smothered seedlings die.

---

<a id="step-w3"></a>
### 💧 Fall Watering Shift
**Timing:** September → October  **Programs:** Supplemental
**Status:** ⬜ Not started

**What to do:**
- **If you overseeded:**
  - Weeks 1–3: **light watering 3×/day** (~5–10 min per zone) to keep the top ½" moist.
  - Weeks 4–6: **once a day**, longer.
  - After that: back to **~1"/week**, deep and infrequent.
- **If you didn't seed:** drop to **~1"/week** including rain. September rains often cover it.
- **Mowing:** return to **3–3.5"**. Keep mowing as long as the grass grows.

---

<a id="step-s5"></a>
### 🌿 Fall Broadleaf Weed Control
**Timing:** Early → Late October  **Programs:** Supplemental (skip if you seeded)
**Status:** ⬜ Not started

**What to do:**
- Fall is the **most effective** time to kill dandelions, clover and ground ivy (creeping Charlie). Weeds are pulling energy down to their roots and take the herbicide with it.
- Spot-spray a 2,4-D / triclopyr blend on a 50–80°F day with no rain for 24 hours.
- **Skip** if you overseeded this fall. It will harm the new grass.

---

<a id="step-s6"></a>
### 🍁 Leaf Management
**Timing:** Mid-October → November (weekly while trees drop)  **Programs:** Supplemental
**Status:** ⬜ Not started

**What to do:**
- **Mulch-mow** light leaf cover into the lawn. It's free organic matter.
- **Rake or bag** heavy layers. Leaves matted on the lawn over winter cause snow mold and kill new seedlings.
- **Most important on newly seeded areas.**

---

<a id="step-5"></a>
### ⛄ Step 5: Snowman®
**Timing:** Late October → November  **Programs:** Both
**Status:** ⬜ Not started

**What to do:**
- Apply Snowman® (winterizer) at the bag rate. It strengthens roots, improves disease resistance and gives an earlier green-up next spring.
- **Timing cue:** after top growth slows but while the grass is still green, usually around the last or second-to-last mow.
- Water in, or apply before rain.
- This is the last product of the season.

---

<a id="step-w4"></a>
### 🔒 Season Close-Out
**Timing:** Mid → Late November  **Programs:** Supplemental
**Status:** ⬜ Not started

**What to do:**
- **Final mow:** lower to **~2.5"** for the last cut, which reduces snow mold and vole damage.
- **Irrigation:** shut it off and **blow out the lines** before the first hard freeze (~mid-November in KC). Disconnect the hoses.
- **Mower:** run it out of gas or add fuel stabilizer, clean the deck, and pull the blade for sharpening now so it's ready in March.
- **Winter watering:** if there's no rain or snow for 4+ weeks (Dec–Feb), water once on a day above 40°F, especially new seedlings.
- **Next season:** reset this tracker to ⬜ at the start of March.

## 📲 iPhone Calendar Sync

`lawn-calendar.ics` is generated from the Status Table by `tools/build_lawn_calendar.py`. It's rebuilt automatically after every update, whether that comes from a one-tap link or from Claude. Each ⬜ step gets:
- **🛒 Buy** reminder about 1 week before the window opens
- **🌱 Window opens** reminder
- **⏰ Last call** reminder 5 days before the window closes

Each one alerts at 9 AM. Its notes link back to its section here and include **✅ Mark done**, **❌ Skip** and **💬 Talk to Claude** links. Steps marked ✅, ❌ or ➖ disappear from the calendar. Next season's events are already included.

**One-time setup (about 1 minute):**
1. iPhone **Settings → Apps → Calendar → Calendar Accounts → Add Account → Other → Add Subscribed Calendar**. On older iOS: Settings → Calendar → Accounts.
2. Server: `https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bwspargo3/Weather-and-Lawn/claude/lawn-care-tracker/lawn-calendar.ics`
3. Tap **Next**, then turn **Remove Alerts OFF**. iOS turns it on by default, and you'd get no notifications.
4. Tap **Save**. Optional: under **Fetch New Data**, set it to *Hourly* so updates show up faster.

**How the one-tap links work:**
- **✅ / ❌** opens a GitHub issue titled like `✅ 4 done`. The date defaults to the day you submit (Central time). Put a different date in the title if needed, e.g. `✅ 4 done (Oct 4)`. Anything you type in the body is saved to the Journal below.
- A GitHub Action (`.github/workflows/lawn-tracker-update.yml`) applies it, rebuilds the calendar, replies on the issue and closes it. Only issues opened by the repo owner are processed.
- **💬 Talk to Claude** opens the Claude Code session for free-form changes (lawn size, plan changes, problems you've spotted).
